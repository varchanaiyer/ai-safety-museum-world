Teaching What We Cannot Grade

In the caseA forty-page proof. Beside it, the grader’s red pen, out of ink.

Suppose the model’s answer would take experts a decade to check, novel research, sprawling code, long plans. A thumbs-up from a hurried human means nothing. Scalable oversight is the search for leverage: models debating models while humans judge the debate; weak teachers supervising strong students; verifying one honest step instead of the whole galaxy of work.

It rests on an old asymmetry, checking is easier than doing, and on a hope the field states plainly: that meaningful human judgment can stay in the loop for minds faster, stranger, and eventually smarter than the judges. This is alignment’s open frontier.
