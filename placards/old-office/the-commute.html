The Commute

In the caseA window seat, 07:42 service, preserved mid-journey. The condensation is original.

Twice daily, the entire working population physically relocated itself, an hour in, an hour out, packed into trains and traffic, to sit at desks identical to the desks at home. At its peak the ritual consumed two hundred billion human hours a year. For many, it ended in a single week in 2020.

The museum preserves it not as absurdity but as testimony: how much a civilization will pay, in hours and in weather, simply to be in the same room as itself. When historians ask what the old office actually produced, the honest answer may be presence. This gallery is what presence looked like.
