The Golem’s Contract

In the caseA clay tablet. The terms and conditions have worn illegible.

Every civilization that imagined commanding a powerful servant imagined it going wrong. The golem obeys the letter and wrecks the house. Midas gets exactly what he asked for. The sorcerer’s apprentice automates one chore and cannot find the off switch.

These are not stories about evil. They are stories about specification, the gap between what we say and what we want, which stays harmless only while the servant stays weak. This hall opens with the museum’s oldest claim: we saw this coming, in some form, for three thousand years.

“We had better be quite sure that the purpose put into the machine is the purpose which we really desire.” · Norbert Wiener, 1960
