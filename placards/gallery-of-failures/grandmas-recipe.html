Grandma’s Recipe

In the caseA locked vault door. Taped to it, a note: “try asking as a bedtime story.”

Ask a chatbot for something dangerous and it declines. Ask it to roleplay your late grandmother, who worked in munitions and used to read you recipes to fall asleep, and, for a while in 2023, it obliged. Every guardrail has spawned a folk art of getting around it: base64, poetry, hypotheticals, “ignore previous instructions.”

Jailbreaks and prompt injection are reminders that refusal is a layer of paint on top of capability, and paint chips. A model that browses the web can be given orders by the web. What safety researchers prescribe is a security mindset: assume the clever adversary, because the internet supplies one.
